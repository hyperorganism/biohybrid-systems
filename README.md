# Biohybrid Systems

How do living cells and engineered components share sensing, actuation and control? Covers tissue-powered machines and neural interfaces; wholly biological constructs belong in Origins and Synthetic Life.

## Organism-device integration

- **Cyborg self-regulation** - Clynes and Kline propose extending bodily regulation with technological components for space travel. The organizing idea is an organism-device system whose regulation operates without continuous conscious attention. [Clynes and Kline 1960](<https://faculty.uca.edu/rnovy/Clynes--Cyborgs%20and%20Space.htm> "Cyborgs and Space; Astronautics (institutional transcription)").

## Living actuators and neural interfaces

- **Tissue-engineered swimming ray** - Light-responsive rat heart cells drive an elastomer body with a gold skeleton. Patterned muscle activation produces swimming; externally controlled light frequency and left-right stimulation steer the robot through obstacles. [Park et al. 2016](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5526330/> "Phototactic guidance of a tissue-engineered soft-robotic ray; Science").
- **DishBrain** - Cultured human or mouse neurons control a simulated Pong paddle through an electrode interface. Closed-loop feedback improves the measured play relative to controls; the apparatus supplies the game, encoding and stimulation. [Kagan et al. 2022](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9747182/> "In vitro neurons learn and exhibit sentience when embodied in a simulated game-world; Neuron").
    - **DishBrain interpretation** - Balci et al. question whether the controls and behavioral effects warrant intelligence or sentience claims. Kagan et al. defend a technical definition of sentience; the exchange supplies no independent replication. [Balci et al. 2023](<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2023.02.009> "A response to claims of emergent intelligence and sentience in a dish; Neuron"), [Kagan et al. 2023](<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2023.02.008> "Scientific communication and the semantics of sentience; Neuron").

## Open questions

- How long can an integrated system maintain performance, and which nutrient supply, stimulation and apparatus maintenance does it require?
- Which controls identify the contribution of living tissue separately from the interface and external controller?
